## 1. BACKGROUND & PROFESSIONAL STANDING
Derek Vance is a seven-year veteran broadcast engineer with Solaris Media. Unlike the glamorous cast, Derek is a quiet, balding, socially awkward computer specialist who prefers staying locked inside the air-conditioned **Master Server Room (Room 14)**. He is married to his high school sweetheart, Sarah, and has two young children (ages 5 and 7) on the mainland. Production notes indicate his family watches the live broadcast every night to "say goodnight to Dad's island."

Despite his clean criminal record, Derek has been under intense professional scrutiny during Season 4. A series of live encoder crashes and audio drop-outs drew a formal termination warning from Mainland Network Executives on **14 October**: one more broadcast failure or production scandal would result in the immediate firing of his entire technical team and the forfeiture of his completion bonus.
