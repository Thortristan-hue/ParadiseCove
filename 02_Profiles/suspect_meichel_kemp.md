## 1. BACKGROUND & PERSONALITY
Meichel (pronounced *Michael*, spelled *M-e-i-c-h-e-l* due to a birth certificate clerical error he defensively corrects everyone on) is an obsessive, detail-oriented camera optics and prop technician. While harmless and deeply nerdy, Meichel struggles with basic social cues and is frequently mocked behind his back by the male contestants when he comes around to swap their microphone batteries.

Meichel takes extreme pride in his work. Especially, his attention to detail for the cleanliness of camera lenses with special wipes, he has ordered from abroad, and his extensive knowledge of every **piece of equipment and props on the set**.

## 2. KEY INCIDENTS & FRICTION POINTS
- **The Lost Keycard:** On **16 October**, Meichel dropped his primary **`STAFF_02`** magnetic keycard somewhere between the Confession Hall and the Pool Deck. Too terrified of Derek Vance's temper to ask for a full security re-keying, Meichel quietly cloned a temporary spare card (**`STAFF_04_TEMP`**) for himself and left **`STAFF_02`** active.
- **Unrequited Awkwardness:** Several crew members noticed Meichel blushing and stammering whenever victim **Elaine Sharkey** visited the tech corridor, though Elaine largely ignored him.
