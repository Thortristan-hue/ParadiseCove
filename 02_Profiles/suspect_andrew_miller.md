## 1. BACKGROUND & VOLATILE HISTORY
Andrew is the least media-trained member of the cast. A working-class line cook recruited for his rugged looks, Andrew genuinely fell in love with **Amber Kors**, though his stoic pride prevents him from expressing it articulately. 

During Weeks 1 and 2, Andrew was briefly coupled with **Elaine Sharkey** before she dumped him for Jordan. Since then, Elaine relentlessly mocked Andrew's background and temper in the **Confession Hall**, baiting him into arguments to make him look unstable on television. Production issued Andrew a formal behavioral warning on Day 51 after he punched a palm tree following one of Elaine's remarks.

## 2. MOTIVE & BLACKOUT NIGHT BEHAVIOR
- By **21:30** on 17 October, Andrew had figured out that Jordan was trying to poach Amber behind his back—and he blamed Elaine and Jordan equally for turning the villa into a snake pit.
- During the blackout party, Andrew consumed nearly an entire bottle of blanco tequila. At **23:00**, multiple contestants heard him shouting near the pool bar that he was going to *"shatter their little fairytale before tomorrow night"* before smashing his champagne flute against the stone coping, lacerating his right knuckles.

## 3. CLAIMED ALIBI (22:00 – 00:00 BLACKOUT)
- Admits he was furious and drunk, and admits he walked toward the **Central Pavilion around 23:05** looking for Jordan or Elaine to confront them.
- Claims he got dizzy in **Lounge 1**, vomited in the planter, and walked back out to the courtyard cabana daybed by **23:15**, where he passed out cold until the cameras turned back on at **00:00**.
