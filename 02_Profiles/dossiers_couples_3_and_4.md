## COUPLE #3: JACKSON COLE (`CAST_05`) & JANA REED (`CAST_06`)

### Background & Dynamic
- **Jackson Cole (23):** Amateur motocross influencer from a wealthy rural family. Arrogant, protective of his image, and terrified of negative press.
- **Jana Reed (21):** Former boutique sales clerk. Completely infatuated with Jackson to an unhealthy, codependent degree; views herself as his fierce protector.

### Hidden Friction & Motive (The Countryside Secret)
Two weeks ago, during a drunken truth-or-dare game, Jackson let slip a town name and date from when he was 17. Elaine Sharkey—whose former flight attendant colleague lived in that exact county—connected the dots to an unsolved **juvenile hit-and-run in the countryside** that left a local teenager critically injured before Jackson's family covered it up. 
- Since Day 50, Elaine has forced Jackson and Jana to vote her way in every ceremony under the threat of dropping the story on a post-show podcast.
- **Alibi Claim:** Jackson and Jana claim they were making out in **Hideaway A (Room 29)** from **22:00 to 23:45**, never entering the Central Pavilion.

## COUPLE #4: CODY BANKS (`CAST_07`) & TRIXXY BELL (`CAST_08`)

Beneath her sugary compliments to Elaine's face, Trixxy despised Elaine with a visceral hatred for humiliating her relationship on international TV.

### Blackout Night Observation
- **Alibi Claim:** Cody and Trixxy spent the blackout arguing and drinking in **Lounge 3** (Central Pavilion) between **22:20 and 23:35**.
- While in Lounge 3, they overheard loud voices coming from the adjacent **Confession Hall (Room 08)** corridor around **22:40**, and noticed specific movement near the service doors, which they are eager to gossip about—mostly to trash Elaine's reputation.
