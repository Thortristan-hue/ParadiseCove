REPORTING OFFICER: Patrolman H. Keller (Badge #884)
DATE/TIME FILED: 18 October 2026 @ 01:40
INCIDENT TYPE: Unattended Death (Preliminary Assessment: Accidental)
================================================================================

1. SCENE ARRIVAL & CONDITION:
Arrived at Room 10 ("Finale Bridal Suite") of the Paradise Cove compound at 00:21
following a landline emergency call from Host Jeanette Hastings. Found female 
victim, identified as contestant ELAINE SHARKEY (DOB: 14-May-2004), lying prone 
(face-down) on the white marble floor near the rear dressing vanity. 

Victim was wearing a floor-length gold sequined evening gown. A large full-length 
freestanding vanity mirror was tipped forward and shattered across the floor 
immediately adjacent to the body. An overturned crystal champagne flute was also 
present near the victim's right hand.

2. PRELIMINARY OBSERVATIONS:
Significant pooling of blood beneath the torso, soaking the front and back of the
gold gown. Upon rolling the victim to check for vital signs, Paramedic Reyes noted
a deep puncture wound in the upper left back (scapular/intercostal region) and a
fresh red contusion across the victim's left cheek/zygomatic arch.

3. INITIAL THEORY OF INCIDENT:
Cast members had been drinking heavily since 21:45 during a two-hour production
camera shutdown. Multiple empty bottles of tequila and champagne were recovered
from the courtyard. Preliminary assessment is that Victim SHARKEY entered Room 10
alone while heavily intoxicated to try on her finale gown, tripped in her 5-inch
stiletto heels, struck her left cheek against the vanity edge, and pulled the 
heavy freestanding mirror down onto her back—causing a lethal puncture from a 
falling mirror shard.

4. ANOMALIES FLAGGED FOR DETECTIVE REVIEW (REASON FOR ESCALATION):
Circuit Coroner Dr. V. Aris arrived at 01:15 and halted removal of the body, 
rejecting my accidental fall hypothesis for the reason of:
     None of the shattered vanity mirror shards on the floor had blood on their 
     edges, and the mirror glass is only 4mm flat sheet glass, which Dr. Aris 
     states could not inflict a narrow 14cm-deep vertical stab channel.

Scene taped off and handed over to Special Maritime & Insular Crimes Unit.
