OFFICER KELLER: Hold your right hand still so the medic can finish wrapping 
those knuckles, Mr. Miller. You want to tell me how you got four gashes across 
your right hand around 23:00 tonight?

ANDREW: I crushed a champagne flute against the pool deck. Ask any of the eight 
people sitting at the bar—Liam, Chloe, Toby, they all watched me do it. I didn't 
stab anybody.

OFFICER KELLER: Multiple witnesses heard you shouting at 23:00 that you were 
going to "shatter their little fairytale before night," and we already 
pulled your 17:15 Confessional Booth tape where you swore on your life that 
Elaine and Jordan wouldn't make it to the finale stage.

ANDREW: Yeah, I said it! Because this whole island is a rigged, plastic joke! 
I'm a line cook from Ohio, man. I came on this show thinking people were real. 
Elaine used me for two weeks, tossed me aside like garbage, and spent the next 
six weeks making fun of my paycheck and my temper in front of a million people. 
And then tonight, right before the blackout, I see Jordan making eyes at Amber—
the one girl here I actually cared about—and I hear Elaine laughing about how 
she's pulling all the strings!

OFFICER KELLER: Where did you go at 23:05 after you smashed the glass?

ANDREW: I went inside the Central Pavilion to find Jordan and knock his teeth 
down his throat. I swiped into Lounge 1 around 23:07. 

OFFICER KELLER: Lounge 1 is thirty feet down the corridor from Room 10 where 
Elaine was killed.

ANDREW: I didn't make it down the corridor! I drank almost a whole fifth of 
blanco tequila by the pool, Officer. The second I stepped into the AC in 
Lounge 1, the room started spinning. I threw up in the fiddle-leaf fig planter 
by the Lounge 1 door, turned right back around, stumbled out to the poolside 
daybed at 23:14, and passed out cold until the floodlights kicked back on at 
midnight. Check the planter in Lounge 1 if you don't believe me! I'm a hothead, 
not a backstabber. If I go after someone, I look 'em in the eyes.
