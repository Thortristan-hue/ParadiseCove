SUBJECT: JEANETTE HASTINGS (STAFF_05 — SHOW HOST & CONSULTING PRODUCER)

LOCATION: Host Executive Suite (Room 09) // DATE: 18 Oct 2026 @ 02:10

OFFICER KELLER: Ms. Hastings, thank you for placing the emergency call at 00:11. 
Your suite here, Room 09, shares a wall directly with Room 10 where Elaine 
Sharkey was killed, and your door faces the Production Service Corridor. Did 
you hear or see anything between 22:00 and midnight?

JEANETTE: (Composed, smoothing blazer) Officer Keller, before I answer, I need 
your assurance that Solaris Media's legal counsel is being looped in. We have 
an active unauthorized mirror stream broadcasting this lockdown to the entire 
internet, and reckless speculation will destroy a hundred-million-dollar 
television franchise.

OFFICER KELLER: A twenty-two-year-old woman was murdered on the other side of 
your bedroom wall, Ms. Hastings. The coroner says it wasn't an accident.

JEANETTE: (Sighs softly) It's a tragedy. Truly. Elaine was... television gold, 
even if she played a dangerous game with everyone around her. As for my 
evening: I landed by helicopter at 21:31, attended the brief announcement at 
21:45, and retired here to Room 09 at 22:15 with chamomile tea and my finale 
monologue cards. The walls in the Central Pavilion are soundproofed for broadcast 
acoustics. I didn't hear a scream.

OFFICER KELLER: You stayed in Room 09 the entire time until 23:54?

JEANETTE: Yes. Well... I did hear a heavy metallic thud or a crash against the 
shared vanity wall around 23:25—I assumed Elaine had knocked over a garment 
rack while trying on her gown. I briefly opened my corridor door a few minutes 
later, around 23:35, just to check if everything was quiet before heading to 
Lounge 2 at 23:54.

OFFICER KELLER: And when you opened your door at 23:35, did you see anyone in 
the corridor?

JEANETTE: (Hesitates) Nobody from the cast. The contestants are animals when 
they drink, Officer—look at Jackson Cole sweating like a cornered rat all week for example. Focus on the 
contestants. Our production staff are quiet professionals. I certainly have no 
reason to believe any of our crew had personal dealings with Ms. Sharkey.
